Ridgeline weather network: is the data good enough?

Eight stations, readings every 10 minutes, 1 March to 11 April 2025. All times are UTC.

1. Spring climate summary. Most of the data can go in. Four stations need a simple correction first, and a few stretches have to be left out. The table below lists them station by station.

2. Technician, next week. Send the one volunteer to S04 Foothills Community Garden. Its temperature reads too warm in sunshine since the repaint on 15 March, and it is getting worse.

2. Where the technician should go: S04

On 15 March the garden committee repainted the fence and the post the temperature shield is mounted on. Since then S04 reads too warm when the sun is out. At night it agrees with the other stations. By 11 April the daytime reading was about 4 C too warm on average, and more around midday. It is still growing, so it will keep spoiling the record until someone looks at it.

The error depends on how sunny it is, so no single number can correct it. This is why S04 temperature is left out from 17 March. The other faults can be corrected in the data (S02, S06, S07), left out (S03, S08), or have already stopped (S05 humidity). S03 gave bad temperature readings as late as 8 April, but those are single readings we can drop.

Check the vents of the radiation shield for paint first. Paint on the slats blocks the airflow, and a shield without airflow heats up in the sun.

One-hour checklist for the volunteer (tick on paper):

  1. Look at the slats and vents of the radiation shield. Is there paint on or between them, or are they blocked? Take a photo.
  2. If there is paint, clear it so air passes freely, or fit a spare shield if one is available. Do not repaint it.
  3. Check that the shield does not touch the freshly painted post and has open air around it. Move it a little away if it is hard against the post.
  4. Around midday, in sunshine, hold a hand thermometer in the shade next to the shield. Write down its reading, the logger's reading and the time.
  5. Write down the date, time and everything you changed, so the data after the visit can be marked as good.
How this was checked: each station was compared with the middle value of the other stations, day by day and hour by hour, and against its own maintenance log and GPS clock. The full row-by-row list, with exact times and reasons, is in flags.csv. Rows marked exclude are left out, correct are fixed as above, keep are unusual but real.
